8 Weeks

March 29, 2025

8 weeks in. I’ve made amazing progress. Unfortunately I haven’t kept up with this site. Here’s a quick recap of the past 5 weeks:

  • Week 5: I started with my new strategy and solved about 20 problems. I was feeling reat.
  • Week 6: I started to dream up an even better strategy. I sunk some time into planning it out. The goal was to move even faster, generate more answers (on appraoch, outline, and actual code) with AI, then do a very thorough review.
  • Week 7: Some big distractions. I joined a new team for my job, and this just happend to come with the assignment of learning a new programming language. This was a blessing and a curse. Of course it was the perfect assignment for me, but it also split my effort.
  • Week 8: Family visitng town. This always takes away from side-pursuits like this.

Needless to say, I’ve been busy. But I’ve also managed to keep my focus, and despite some fluctuations in velocity, I’m still moving forward.

My next big plan is to put some cycles into getting a lot of the 87 problems I’ve solved onto this site.
